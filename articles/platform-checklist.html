What to Check Before Using an Online Gaming Platform

Use this checklist to review terms, payments, privacy and local laws before sharing personal information or money.

Read the terms

Look for rules about eligibility, bonuses, restrictions, account closure and dispute handling.

Check payments

Confirm payment methods, limits, processing conditions and fees. Avoid relying on unverified payout promises.

Understand privacy

Read the privacy policy, use strong unique passwords and never share one-time codes or account credentials.

Confirm local legality

Online gaming and betting rules differ by jurisdiction and can change. Check current official guidance.

Remember the risks

Losses are possible. Set limits, take breaks and stop if participation affects your wellbeing.
