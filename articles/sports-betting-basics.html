A Beginner’s Guide to Sports Betting Terms

Learn common sports betting terms and odds formats. Sports outcomes remain uncertain and no odds format guarantees a winning result.

What are odds?

Odds express a price or implied probability for an outcome. Formats vary, so check how a platform displays them.

What is a market?

A market is a specific question about an event, such as a team result or total score. Available markets depend on the event.

Why terms matter

Settlement rules for ties, cancellations and abandoned events can differ. Read the rules before making a decision.

Make informed choices

Betting carries financial risk. Do not treat it as income or chase losses. Check local laws and age requirements.
