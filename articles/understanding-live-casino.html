Understanding Live Casino Games: A Beginner’s Guide

Live casino games use video streams to present dealer-led table games online. Learn the rules and check local laws before participating.

What is a live casino?

A live casino typically streams a real dealer or host to an online interface. Game types and availability vary by platform and location.

Read the rules first

Each game has its own rules, limits and pace. Understand how a round works; no action guarantees a result.

Check platform details

Review terms, privacy information, payment conditions and licensing details where applicable. Features may change over time.

Keep play responsible

Treat gaming as entertainment, not income. Set a firm budget, do not borrow to play and take breaks.
